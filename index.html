Frequently Asked Questions

Are Pixwora stickers waterproof?
Yes, all Pixwora stickers are printed on durable vinyl with protective matte lamination, making them 100% waterproof, UV-resistant, and residue-free when peeled.
What paper is used for wall posters?
Our wall posters are printed on heavy 300 GSM premium matte art paper with rich archival inks in standard A5, A4, and A3 sizes.
Where does Pixwora ship from?
Orders are crafted and dispatched from Hyderabad, Telangana, with tracked pan-India delivery to over 24,000 pincodes.
